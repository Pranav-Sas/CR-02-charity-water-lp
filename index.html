Our Story

charity: water is a nonprofit organization bringing clean and safe drinking water to people in developing countries. Since 2006, we've funded over 150,000 water projects in 29 countries, helping more than 17 million people gain access to clean water. 100% of public donations go directly to water projects — we cover our operating costs separately.

Real Impact: Meet Amina

Amina used to walk miles every day to collect water for her family. Thanks to your support, her village now has a clean water well. Amina can go to school, and her family is healthier and happier than ever before.
